Private Google Workspace integration

Barnabas Workspace Connector

A private, single-user integration that allows the account owner’s self-hosted Hermes assistant to work with authorized Google Workspace services.

Purpose

The connector supports owner-directed workflows involving Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Docs, and Google Sheets. It is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts or services to third parties.

Operating principles

  • Access is limited to Google accounts explicitly authorized by their owner.
  • Google user data is used only to perform owner-requested features and automations.
  • Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account security settings.
